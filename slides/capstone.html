Every number in the notes comes out of a seeded rerun. The pre-registration is committed before the experiment log is opened.

Walk through each criterion with one example of full marks, from the rubric in the capstone folder. Design: a decision with a number in it and the right unit with its reason. Power: a simulation that reproduces the formula within its counting error. Analysis: ratio check and balance first. Review: each flaw with what it does to the reported number.

Format

8 minutes for the defence, then 4 minutes of questions

1 · The decision

The case, the decision in one sentence with its number, and the unit you randomise with the reason.

2 · One design choice

The unit, the size or the duration: what you chose, the evidence from your notebooks, and a live rerun with the seed.

3 · The reading

Checks first, then the estimate with its interval against the minimum effect, and what the write-up got wrong.

In the questions, the assessor changes the minimum effect or the unit. You say what happens to the size and the duration, then recompute and show it.

The prediction comes before the recomputation and is scored on its reasoning: half the effect, four times the size; a cluster unit, the design effect. No assistant during the defence or the questions. Peer feedback after each defence: one strength, one question, one suggestion.

Before the first defence

Final prep · 20 minutes

Rerun both notebooks from the raw files, check that the numbers match your notes, and choose the design choice you will defend.

Press T to start the timer. Breaks are not slides: press B when the room needs one. A notebook that gives different numbers on the rerun is the most common loss of marks; a fixed seed prevents it.
