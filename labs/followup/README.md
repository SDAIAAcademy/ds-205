# Follow-up task: the design checklist on an experiment at work

Within three weeks of the capstone day, apply the design checklist to one real or planned
experiment at your workplace. The task is tracked: your trainer records your page and gives
written feedback.

## The task

1. Choose one change your team has tested, is testing or wants to test: a message, a form, a
   process, a default. If nothing has been tested, choose a change that was adopted on the
   strength of a before-and-after comparison.
2. Fill `../prereg_template.md` for it, as it was run or as you would run it.
3. Answer the twelve questions of `../checklist.md` in one or two sentences each. Where the
   honest answer is "we did not do this", write that, and what it would have taken.
4. Compute the sample size for the minimum effect your manager would act on, by formula, and
   say how long it takes at your traffic. If the unit is a branch, a team or a school, say how
   many units you have and what that implies.
5. End with one paragraph: the decision the evidence supports today, and the smallest next
   step that would make it stronger.

## What you hand in

Two pages at most: the filled template, the twelve answers, the sample size with its inputs,
and the closing paragraph. No data.

## Rules

- Hand in no personal data and no record of an individual. Describe data by field names and
  counts only.
- Ask your manager before describing a test that is not public inside your organisation, and
  your data protection officer before any log is extracted for it.
- An assistant may be used; say where you used one. The judgement on each checklist item is
  yours.
