# Pre-registration: one page

Fill every line before the first unit is assigned. Date it. An amendment is allowed before the
test starts, with its own date and reason; after the start, additions are labelled exploratory.
The complete page of the reminder test is `solutions/prereg_reference.json`.

**Title and date:**

**Decision.** Who decides, and what they will do if the effect reaches the minimum, and if it does not:

**Hypothesis.** The change, the metric, the direction:

**Arms.** Who gets what in each arm, and how units are put into arms:

**Why withholding is acceptable.**

**Unit of randomisation,** and why no person can land in both arms:

**Primary metric** (one), with its definition and time window:

**Minimum effect of interest,** in points, with its reason:

**Secondary metrics** (reported whatever they show):

**Guardrails.** For each: metric, threshold, action. One of them protects a named group:

**Stop rule.** The planned end; what may end the test sooner; no early stop for a good result:

**Sample size per arm,** and how it was computed (formula and simulation):

**Duration in days.** Whole weeks; which month-end it covers; when the outcome is complete:

**Analysis plan.** The test, the interval, the unit of analysis, analysed as assigned; subgroups named and labelled exploratory:

**Checks before the result is read.** Sample ratio (the test and its line), balance (the covariates):

**Data.** Fields used; how units are pseudonymised; aggregate reporting; when the extract is deleted; who signed:
